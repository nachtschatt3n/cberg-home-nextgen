**How each limb FAILS, dry-tested 2026-09-21 on a scratch copy of the live log

- **GATE — positive control.** On the live 2.38.7 log the gate reads **0**.

  gate read green for the wrong reason. It is deterministic: `n8n ready on ::,
  port 5678` is line 3 of every boot (measured on the running pod).

> not a PVC delete or a restore-to-new-volume. **Do not delete or recreate
> `n8n-config` (PVC or PV).** PV `n8n-config` is `Retain`, `longhorn-static`,

### 6.5 What happens to the OPEN findings when this ships

Re-queried 2026-09-25 (`finding list --grep n8n`):

- **`F-910a4a4b` (security, AR-029 row) WILL auto-close.** Its title carries a
  backticked `` `n8nio/n8n:2.38.7` ``, and `findings_writer.fingerprint()`
  anchors identity on backticked identifiers — once the image is 2.40.7 the
  2.38.7 row stops being emitted and resolves; any residual re-files under a

  i.e. the **pre-release** line we deliberately do not chase. **Do not "fix"
  this by re-targeting at 2.41.x.** It stays open until the oracle learns the
  channel or 2.41.x is promoted.
- **`F-8e5e5c66` (chart 2.0.1 → 2.1.1, new 2026-09-25)** is untouched by this
